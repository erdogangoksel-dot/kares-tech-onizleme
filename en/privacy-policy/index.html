At KARES TECH ("Company", "we"), we care about the security of your personal data. This policy explains, under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK"), for which purposes your personal data is processed, who it is shared with, and your rights.

For the detailed information required by Article 10 of KVKK, see the KVKK Privacy Notice; for cookies, see the Cookie Policy.

2. Personal data processed

  • Identity and contact details: first name, last name, e-mail, phone, business/school name.
  • Request and form details: the information you submit in pre-registration, demo request, support request and application forms.
  • Account details: account, session and activity records in the customer and staff portals; the information needed for contracts and invoicing with our customers.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser information and cookie data about how the site is used.

Nothing is sold through the site, so no card or payment details are collected on the site.

3. Purposes of processing

  • Informing you about our products and services and handling demo/pre-registration requests,
  • Managing and resolving support and service requests,
  • Evaluating reseller and consultant applications, managing customer and staff accounts, performing contracts and invoicing,
  • Protecting the site and accounts against misuse,
  • Meeting legal obligations and improving service quality.

We do not use our customers' data to train artificial intelligence models.

4. Legal grounds for processing

Your personal data is processed on the legal grounds of the formation or performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, legitimate interest, and your explicit consent (for example, consent to commercial communications).

5. Data transfers

Your data may be shared, only as far as the service requires and in line with the law, with authorised public bodies and with the suppliers we use (hosting, e-mail delivery, bot protection and, with your consent, analytics). Your data is not sold to third parties for marketing.

The recipient groups and transfers abroad are described in detail in the KVKK Privacy Notice.

6. Retention period

Your personal data is kept for as long as the purpose of processing requires and for the limitation periods set by the applicable law; at the end of that period it is deleted, destroyed or anonymised. Retention periods per category are listed in the KVKK Privacy Notice.

7. Your rights under KVKK

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information if it has been processed, to learn the purpose of processing, to request the correction, deletion or destruction of incomplete or incorrectly processed data, and to claim compensation for damage if the processing is unlawful.

You can send your requests to info@kares.tech.

8. Cookies

Our site uses essential cookies for session management and to improve the user experience. You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

No non-essential cookie is loaded without your consent. Details, and how to change your choice, are in the Cookie Policy.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when needed. The current version is always published on this page. For questions, contact info@kares.tech.

10. How we protect your data

  • All connections between the site and your browser are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • There is no direct database access from the browser; every request goes through an authorisation check on the server.
  • The Turkish ID number (T.C. kimlik numarası), date of birth, bank details and emergency contact in consultant applications are additionally encrypted; only authorised administrators can see them, and these fields are never sent by e-mail.
  • Staff access is limited by role; everyone sees only the data their job requires.

11. Data processed in our products

For organisations (schools, companies, residential site management) that use our products such as KARES TURNİKE, Kantin, Shift and HOME, the organisation is the data controller for the student, staff and resident data processed there; KARES TECH acts as data processor for that data under the contract with the organisation. Requests about this data should first be sent to the organisation concerned.
