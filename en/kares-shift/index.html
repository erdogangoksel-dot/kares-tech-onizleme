QR-based staff time tracking

No fingerprints. Time tracking by QR, KVKK compliant.

Fingerprint and face recognition mean special-category personal data for time tracking. KARES Shift works without biometrics: at the entrance the employee scans the rotating QR code on the kiosk screen with their phone, and the record is verified on the server. A multi-branch, role-based time tracking platform that keeps working when the internet drops.

From biometrics to QR

You should not need to hand over your face or fingerprint to track working hours.

Biometric systems

  • Fingerprint, face, palm or iris: special-category personal data is collected.
  • Storing, protecting and deleting that data puts a heavy burden on the employer.
  • Reader hardware at the door breaks down, gets dirty and needs replacing.

KARES Shift (QR)

  • No biometric data is collected; the employee uses their own phone, logged in to their own account.
  • The rotating, signed QR code is verified on the server; a copied code is useless.
  • There is only a kiosk screen at the door; no reader hardware is needed.

KVKK

Works with the methods the Board accepts

With decision no. 2026/921 of 2 June 2026, the KVKK Board (Turkish Personal Data Protection Board) prohibited processing biometric data for time tracking, even with the employee's consent. The alternatives listed in the decision are QR codes, RFID/NFC cards, PINs, digital signatures and manual entry under a manager's supervision.

  • QR code: KARES Shift's primary method.
  • RFID / NFC card, PIN, digital signature and supervised manual entry: the other methods named in the decision.
  • Biometric data is never collected or stored at any stage.

This section is not legal advice; consult your legal adviser about your own situation.

KARES Shift

How it works

  1. 01

    Step 1: The kiosk shows a QR code

    The Windows kiosk screen at the entrance shows a signed QR code; it refreshes every 60 seconds, so a screenshot cannot be used to cheat.

  2. 02

    Step 2: The employee scans

    The employee scans the code with the iOS or Android app. Without internet the record waits on the phone and is sent when the connection returns.

  3. 03

    Step 3: The server verifies

    Only the server writes entry and exit records; the code, device and branch are verified, and a suspicious record is flagged for the manager.

  4. 04

    Step 4: The timesheet is ready

    Absences, late arrivals and overtime show up on the panel in real time; the monthly timesheet for payroll is exported as Excel, PDF or CSV.

KARES Shift

What it does

  • Rotating QR code

    The kiosk QR code refreshes every 60 seconds; the signed code is verified on the server and bound to the device. A photo of the code sent to someone else is useless.

  • Multi-branch structure

    One group, many branches, each branch with its own kiosk. Each company's data is kept separate; the rules are enforced at the database level.

  • Role-based access

    Five permission levels for general manager, HR manager, branch manager, department lead and employee. Everyone only sees the data they are authorised to see.

  • Even when the internet drops

    The mobile app keeps scanning offline; records sync when the network returns. No time record is lost.

  • Web control panel

    Absences, late arrivals, overtime and department trends in real time. Reports export as Excel and PDF.

  • Windows kiosk mode

    A large, rotating QR screen at the factory entrance. Employees scan with their own phones; no reader hardware is needed.

  • Tamper-proof audit trail

    Approvals, corrections, consent and deletion events go into an append-only log: who, when, from which device, approved by whom.

  • Payroll-ready output

    Monthly absences and hours export as CSV, ready for payroll. No need to compile Excel sheets by hand.

  • KVKK tools

    The app asks for explicit consent at first login and stores it with a version; for the right to be forgotten an employee's data is deleted in one step, and the deletion stays in the audit trail.

Role-based access

Five levels; everyone only sees their own data.

  • General manager

    • All branches, HR and payroll
    • Real-time visibility across the group

  • HR manager

    • All branches, reports and approvals
    • Exports the audit trail for the auditor

  • Branch manager

    • Only their own branch and staff
    • Time approvals and corrections

  • Department lead

    • Only their own department
    • Their team's time status

  • Employee

    • Only their own entry history
    • Can submit a time correction request

Privacy and security

Minimal data for time tracking

KARES Shift collects no biometric data; the data processed and the purposes for keeping it are defined up front.

KVKK privacy notice

  • Data processed: name, e-mail and role; entry and exit time, branch and device ID; approval requests and consent records.
  • Location is processed only if an area is defined for the branch, and only at the moment of entry; there is no continuous tracking.
  • Each company's data is separate; cross-access is denied by database rules, and these rules are verified by automated tests.
  • Only the server writes time, approval, audit and consent records; they cannot be changed from the app.
  • Explicit consent is stored with a version and can be withdrawn; under the right to be forgotten the employee's data is deleted, and the deletion is logged without any personal data.

Frequently asked questions

Why QR instead of a biometric system?

Fingerprints and face recognition are special-category personal data, and using them for time tracking brings a heavy KVKK (Turkish data protection law) burden. With QR no biometric data is ever collected; what identifies the employee is their own phone, logged in to their own account.

What if an employee photographs the QR code and sends it to a colleague?

It will not work. The code refreshes every 60 seconds, is signed and is verified on the server; a scan is bound to the employee's own device. Unusual records are flagged and go to the manager for review.

What happens if the internet goes down at the factory?

The mobile app keeps scanning; records wait on the phone and are sent to the server when the connection returns.

Who sees which data?

There are five permission levels. The general manager and HR see all branches, a branch manager only their own branch, a department lead their own team, and an employee only their own entry history.

Is our location tracked all the time?

No. Location is only checked if an area is defined for the branch, and only at the moment of entry; there is no continuous tracking.

How does it get into payroll?

Monthly absences and hours export as CSV; Excel and PDF reports are also available. Integration with your existing HR or accounting system is discussed during the rollout.

How long does the rollout take?

The planned flow is four weeks: setup, a pilot at one branch, rollout to all branches and fine-tuning. Records are kept by QR from the first week.
