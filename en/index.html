KARES OCR

Photograph the document. It ends up in Mikro.

KARES OCR reads invoices, receipts and utility bills with AI and writes them to the Mikro ERP together with the supplier transaction and the accounting entry, following your chart of accounts. Your accountant reviews a prepared entry instead of filling in fields.

  • Reads e-invoices, e-archive invoices, receipts, utility bills, invoices with withholding tax and freelance receipts by document type
  • Posts through Mikro's official API as a purchase invoice or a journal voucher
  • Duplicate, arithmetic, period and currency checks stop a wrong entry before it reaches the books
  • Nothing is written to Mikro without approval

How we work

Why KARES

Why KARES?
  • 01

    Proof before go-live

    Every project starts with a trial on your own data. With KARES OCR, entries are compared one by one with your accountant's own records before going live.

  • 02

    People make the decisions

    Automation takes over the mechanical work; approval stays with your team. Suggestions are never applied silently, and uncertain fields are flagged.

  • 03

    Your data, your infrastructure

    KARES OCR runs on your own server, KARES HOME data stays in the building and KARES Kantin runs on the school's local network. We state clearly which data goes where.

  • 04

    Keeps working offline

    Our turnstile, canteen, andon and building products are designed to run on the local network on site; records sync when the connection returns.
