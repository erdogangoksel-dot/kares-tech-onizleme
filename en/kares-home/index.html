Smart building platform

The whole estate on one platform, with its brain inside the building.

Door access from one vendor, parking from another, home automation from a third, each with its own app and its own cloud. KARES HOME brings access, visitors, parking, smart living and shared amenities together on a single server inside the building. If the internet goes down, the building keeps working, and residents' data stays on site.

KARES HOME

Who it is for

  • Developers

    A smart estate offering that sets the project apart, delivered by one vendor under one contract, with the network design included.

  • Residential estates

    Gates, visitors, parking and shared amenities managed from one live panel instead of several unconnected systems.

  • Residences

    Wall panel living with security and visitor handling that residents control from their phone.

  • Gated and villa communities

    Gates, parking, pool and homes run on the same logic and the same app.

KARES HOME

How it works

  1. 01

    Step 1: Scope the project

    Together we choose the modules for the project: from access only to the complete estate platform. The scope is part of the contract.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Install the building server

    The first device installed is the estate's own server. Panels, kiosks, phones and locks connect to it over the local network.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Switch modules on in stages

    Pedestrian access comes first; parking, smart home and amenities follow as construction stages are completed, without reinstalling anything.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Train and support

    We train the management team and stay with you after handover, with remote updates and on-site support.

KARES HOME

What it does

  • Access module

    Two-way visitor approval, dynamic QR codes and NFC for residents, door kiosks, locks and turnstiles. No biometrics.

  • Parking

    Vehicle recognition by number plate, RFID or Bluetooth, separate flows for residents and visitors, and visitor entry by code.

  • Smart home

    Lighting, climate, scenes, schedules and an energy overview for each apartment, on the wall panel and on the phone.

  • Pool and amenities

    Opening hours, capacity limits and guest rules for shared amenities, with QR entry.

  • Maintenance

    Fault records, a planned maintenance calendar and contractor visits linked to access control.

  • Modules by licence

    Every capability is a module enabled by the project's licence. A module can be added years later without reinstalling; switching one off sets its data aside instead of deleting it.

KARES HOME

Works with

  • S

    Standard hardware

    Tablets as wall panels and door kiosks, residents' iOS and Android phones, barriers, plate recognition cameras, RFID readers and controller-based locks.

  • B

    Building server

    One on-site server makes every decision. The cloud is only used for licence checks, encrypted backups and notifications, and never opens a door.

Privacy and security

Residents' data stays in the building

Privacy is part of the design: we collect as little personal data as possible and keep it on site.

KVKK privacy notice (in Turkish)

  • No biometric data: residents and visitors use QR codes, NFC or approval on the phone
  • Number plates are stored only as irreversible keyed hashes
  • Optional cloud backups are encrypted before they leave the building
  • Devices on the local network connect with their own certificates; the server only makes outbound connections
  • [DRAFT] Every critical event (entries, approvals, changes) is kept in a tamper-evident audit trail
  • If the server fails, doors and kiosks fall back to cached rules: exits open, entrances stay locked, as fire regulations require

KARES HOME

Frequently asked questions

What happens if the internet goes down?

The building keeps working. Approvals, entries, scenes and records run on the local network through the on-site server; when the connection returns, the cloud side simply catches up.

Where is residents' data stored?

On the server inside the building. Optional backups are encrypted before they leave the building, so the cloud never sees plain data.

Do we have to take every module at once?

No. The scope is defined per project, from access only to the complete estate platform. Further modules can be switched on later without reinstalling.

Does KARES HOME use face or fingerprint recognition?

No. Identity is handled with QR codes, NFC and approval on the resident's phone; no biometric data is collected.
