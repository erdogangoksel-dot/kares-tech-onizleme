Point-of-sale app for cafes and restaurants

Your till in the palm of your hand.

An Android management app that needs no internet connection, for cafes, restaurants, bars, patisseries, eateries, kebab and burger shops. It runs on a phone or tablet: tables, orders, payments, customer tabs, recipes and a QR menu on one screen.

Get it on Google PlayRequest a demo

KARESBAR LITE

For every food and drink business

  • Cafes and coffee houses

    Table service, a wide range of drinks and fast order tickets.

  • Restaurants and eateries

    Table plan, order transfer and split bills.

  • Bars and bistros

    Fast orders at peak hours and ready-made cocktail recipes.

  • Patisseries and dessert shops

    Counter / self-service mode and a ready-made patisserie menu template.

  • Fast food and kiosks

    Ready-made menus for kebab, burgers, chicken and rice.

  • Any small business

    No till system or POS hardware needed; one Android device is enough.

KARESBAR LITE

How it works

  1. 01

    Step 1: Download and open

    Download the app from Google Play. No installation or technician needed; set your admin password on first launch.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Pick a menu template

    Cafe, bar, restaurant, kebab, burger shop, chicken and rice, eatery, dessert shop or patisserie: your menu loads with one tap.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Set up your tables

    Set up your table layout once; see which tables are occupied at a glance every day.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Start taking orders

    Orders, payments and tickets on the same screen; with a compatible thermal printer the receipt prints in seconds.

KARESBAR LITE

What it does

  • Works offline

    Work goes on even when the Wi-Fi drops. Data is stored on your device and never sent to a server.

  • Table management

    Open, occupied and available tables are colour-coded; order transfer, merging bills, table service and counter / kiosk mode.

  • Fast orders, flexible payment

    Products listed by category, variant support, full and partial payment, and payment methods you define yourself.

  • Customer tabs

    Customer profiles, payment history per transaction, partial payments against the balance and the total open balance: no paper ledger needed.

  • 106+ ready-made recipes

    Ready-made recipes from coffee to desserts, soups to burgers; add your own and pass your kitchen standard on to new staff.

  • QR menu

    Create a mobile-friendly menu from your product list and print the QR code on the thermal printer; the QR stays the same when the menu changes.

  • Reports

    Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly sales summaries, best sellers and the split by payment method; all reports in one PDF.

  • Expenses and suppliers

    Rent, electricity, supplies: record expenses by category, track what you owe suppliers and see the monthly profit or loss.

  • Staff and activity log

    Unlimited staff accounts; the admin manages everything, staff see the order screen. The activity log always shows who did what.

Privacy and security

Your data stays on your device

KARESBAR LITE uses a local database; day-to-day use does not depend on the internet or a server.

KARESBAR LITE Privacy Policy

  • Sales, table and customer tab data are stored on the device; nothing is sent to a server and there is no third-party analytics.
  • Backups are taken as ZIP files; passwords appear in a backup only as one-way hashes.
  • Admin and staff roles are separate; the activity log shows who did what.

KARESBAR LITE

Frequently asked questions

Does KARESBAR LITE work if the internet goes down?

Yes. Every feature works without an internet connection; data is stored on the device.

Which devices does it run on?

Android 5.0 or later phones and tablets. No till system or POS hardware is needed.

How do I move my data to a new device?

Back up your data as a ZIP from the Settings menu and restore it from the same menu on the new device. The steps are described in the knowledge base (in Turkish).

The knowledge base (in Turkish) has 25 guides, from first launch to backups. If you would like help with setup or a live demo, call or e-mail us and we will get back to you.
