ERP Solutions

Our services

  • AI document automation

    Invoices, receipts, utility bills and self-employment receipts are read, checked and prepared as accounting entries in the ERP. Our ready-made product for Mikro is KARES OCR.

  • ERP integration

    Two-way connection between your ERP and your other systems through the official API or service layer: syncing customer and supplier accounts, stock, orders and invoices; multi-company and multi-branch setups; protection against errors and duplicate records.

  • e-Transformation compliance

    Setting up e-Invoice, e-Archive, e-Dispatch, e-Ledger, e-SMM (self-employment receipt) and e-Müstahsil (producer receipt) processes together with your ERP and your private integrator; obligation check, connection test and regulatory follow-up.

  • Marketplaces and e-commerce

    Orders from Trendyol, Hepsiburada, Amazon, n11 and your own website flowing into the ERP: order, dispatch note and invoice; stock and product data sync; return and commission entries.

  • Bank and POS integration

    Bank account transactions and POS collections transferred to the ERP: one screen for many banks, rule-based account matching and a reconciliation report.

  • Custom module development

    An add-on application (desktop, web or mobile) working with ERP data for what your ERP does not do: approval flows, purchase requests, service, production and project tracking, construction site and field apps.

  • Field sales and dealer portal

    Sales reps and dealers see orders, collections and account statements on the phone, up to date with the ERP: offline orders with later sync, dealer-specific terms, routes and visit logs.

  • Reporting and management dashboard

    Live management dashboards from ERP data; the report a manager wants in one click or by scheduled e-mail: cash flow, credit risk and ageing, sales and profitability analysis; role-based and mobile-friendly.

  • Data migration and transition

    Controlled transfer from an old program, Excel or another ERP (customer and supplier accounts, stock, opening balances), a validation report and a parallel-run period; planning for the move from server to cloud.

Where ERP is heading in Türkiye

The market is changing; an ERP alone is no longer enough.

Six topics have set the ERP agenda for businesses over the last two years. We built our services around them.

  • e-Transformation

    Thresholds are falling, scope is widening

    The general gross sales threshold for e-Invoice (e-Fatura) and e-Ledger (e-Defter) is currently 3 million lira, and lower in some sectors. e-Archive has become widespread, and e-Dispatch (e-İrsaliye) is mandatory above certain turnover levels and in certain sectors. Thresholds change with GİB (Turkish Revenue Administration) announcements; we check your situation with you before any setup.

  • Cloud

    The move from servers to the cloud is speeding up

    Turkish ERP vendors are shifting toward cloud and subscription models. Most businesses, however, are in a hybrid phase: part on their own server, part in the cloud.

  • AI

    Data entry is becoming automatic

    Reading without templates of invoices and receipts is now routine. The difference is not the reading itself, but writing the data into the ERP with the right accounts, after it has been checked.

  • Marketplaces

    Orders, stock and invoices between two systems

    Every business that sells online moves orders, stock and invoices between the marketplace and the ERP. Without a connection, this is done by hand every day.

  • Open banking

    Bank transactions flow into the ERP

    Through the banks' API services, account transactions, POS and collections can land in the ERP automatically; matching to customer and supplier accounts and reconciliation take minutes instead of days.

  • KVKK

    Where is the data kept?

    The rules on transferring data abroad under KVKK (Turkish data protection law) have become stricter. "Our data stays on our own server, in Türkiye" is now a deciding question in purchasing.

Privacy and security

Respect for the ERP's rules and your data

The same principle in every job: the ERP's own rules and official interfaces. By default, the data stays in your environment.

KVKK privacy notice

  • We always prefer the vendor's official API or service layer; period locks, permissions and the ERP's own rules stay in force for every record.
  • Direct database access is used for reading only, and only with your approval.
  • The default installation is on your server or in a cloud environment opened in your name; ERP connection details and records stay in your network.
  • We document the KVKK (Turkish data protection law) notices and data processing steps together with you.
  • Before going live, every job is tried in a test company with your real data.

Frequently asked questions

Do we have to replace our ERP?

No. Our solutions work alongside your existing ERP and talk to it through its official interfaces. The goal is not to replace the ERP but to remove the manual work around it.

Is document reading available for ERPs other than Mikro?

KARES OCR is a ready-made product for Mikro today. The same reading and checking approach can be adapted for Logo, Netsis and other ERPs on a project basis; we assess it for your ERP and document volume in the pre-analysis.

Do you write directly to the ERP database?

We always prefer the vendor's official API or service layer, so the ERP's period locks, permissions and own rules stay in force for every record. Direct database access is used for reading only, and only with your approval.

Where is our data kept?

The default installation is on your server or in a cloud environment opened in your name. ERP connection details and records stay in your network; we document the KVKK notices and data processing steps together with you.

How long does a project take?

Installing a ready-made product takes days, integrations usually a few weeks; for custom modules the time depends on the scope. After the pre-analysis we share the scope and timeline in writing.

Can you work with our current ERP consultant?

Yes. Your ERP consultant or reseller stays in charge of installation and licensing; we take on the integration and add-on software. We work to a shared test plan.

Are KARES MİZAN and KARES FATURA ready to use?

KARES MİZAN is in pilot; we are taking pilot and demo requests. KARES FATURA is in development; leave a pre-registration and we will let you know when it is ready. We give no dates for either.
