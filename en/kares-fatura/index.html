KARES FATURA · eBelgem mobile app

e-Archive invoices and receipts, in your pocket.

Self-employed professionals and small businesses usually issue e-Archive invoices and self-employment receipts on the GİB (Turkish Revenue Administration) e-Archive Portal, sitting at a computer. KARES FATURA is being developed so you can use the same portal with your own login details from one app on Android, iOS and the web: prepare the document, sign it with GİB's SMS code and see its PDF right away.

KARES FATURA

Who are we building it for?

  • Self-employed professionals

    Consultants, lawyers, engineers, designers: issue the e-SMM (self-employment receipt) from your phone while you are with the client.

  • Small businesses using the e-Archive Portal

    If you already issue your invoices on the GİB e-Archive Portal, do the same work in fewer steps, on mobile and on the web.

  • People working in the field and on the road

    Reach the invoice list, PDFs and customer details even on a weak connection; the list updates when the connection returns.

KARES FATURA

How it works

  1. 01

    Step 1: Connect your portal login

    Enter your GİB e-Archive Portal login details once; your password is stored encrypted.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Prepare the document

    Create the e-Archive invoice or e-SMM receipt; pick the customer from your saved customers.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Sign with the SMS code

    The document is signed with the SMS approval code GİB sends to your phone.

  4. 04

    Step 4: View and keep the PDF

    View and download the PDF of the issued document; all your documents can be filtered and searched in the list.

KARES FATURA

What the app will do

  • e-Archive invoice

    Creating e-Archive invoices on the GİB e-Archive Portal, with your account.

  • e-SMM receipt

    Issuing the self-employment receipt from the same app, with the same flow.

  • Signing with GİB SMS approval

    Signing uses the one-time SMS code GİB sends to your phone.

  • Invoice list, filter and search

    All your documents in one list: filter, search, page through.

  • PDF view and download

    The PDF of every document opens in the app; you can download and keep it or pass it on.

  • Cancellation

    You can cancel an invoice you issued through the portal from the app.

  • Customer management

    Save your customers once; you do not have to retype their details on a new document.

  • Offline viewing

    The invoice list and customers are stored on the device; they can be viewed without a connection and sync when it returns.

  • Android, iOS and web

    One app: the same account and the same documents on phone, tablet and browser.

Important information

  • KARES FATURA is in development; the features on this page describe the planned scope and may change before release.
  • The GİB e-Archive Portal belongs to the Turkish Revenue Administration (Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı). KARES FATURA is not an app developed or approved by GİB.

KARES FATURA

Portal connection

  • G

    GİB e-Archive Portal

    The app works on the e-Archive Portal of the Turkish Revenue Administration (Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı) with your portal login details. Documents are created on the portal under your account.

Privacy and security

Your portal login is protected

The app works with your GİB e-Archive Portal account; signing always uses the code sent to your phone.

KVKK privacy notice

  • Your GİB e-Archive Portal password is stored encrypted.
  • A document is not signed until the SMS code GİB sends to your phone has been entered.
  • Each user sees only their own documents and customers.
  • The list and customer details kept for offline viewing are stored on your device.

KARES FATURA

Frequently asked questions

When will KARES FATURA be available?

The app is in development; it cannot be downloaded yet and we do not give a release date. Pre-register and we will share it with you first when it is ready.

Which documents will I be able to issue?

e-Archive invoices and e-SMM (self-employment receipts). e-Invoice (e-Fatura), e-Dispatch (e-İrsaliye) and e-Ledger (e-Defter) are outside the app's scope.

Do I need a GİB e-Archive Portal account?

Yes. KARES FATURA uses the portal with your login details; documents are created on the portal under your account and signed with the SMS code GİB sends you.

Is my portal password safe?

Your password is stored encrypted and is used only to carry out actions on the portal. Signing additionally requires the SMS code sent to your phone.

Does it work without internet?

The invoice list and your customers are stored on the device, so they can be viewed without a connection and sync when it returns. Issuing and signing a new document needs an internet connection.

Can I cancel an invoice I issued?

Yes. You can cancel an invoice you issued through the portal from the app.
