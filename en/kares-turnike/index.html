School entry and attendance system

The student taps a card; parents know the same morning.

Attendance sheets are collected by hand, parents hear about absences in the evening, and e-Okul entries are typed in at the end of the day. KARES TURNİKE brings entry control, attendance tracking and parent notification into one system: late and absent students are identified automatically against the school bell, parents get an SMS in the school's name, and the office gets its report with one click.

From a single-entrance school to a multi-gate campus

  • Primary, middle and high schools

    The same system from a small school with one entrance to a large campus with several gates.

  • Vocational and technical high schools

    Several turnstiles in one system for workshop buildings and separate entrances.

  • Private schools and colleges

    Schools where communication with parents is part of the institution's trust.

  • Private tutoring centres

    Institutions that run on lesson-based timetables and a flexible calendar.

The problem

Attendance is still tracked on paper.

Three things get lost along the way: time, accuracy and parents' trust. KARES TURNİKE gives all three back.

Today

  • The duty teacher collects attendance sheets class by class.
  • Late arrivals are only noticed once they walk into class.
  • Parents hear about absences in the evening or on report card day.
  • The secretary types into e-Okul one by one, reading from the screen.

With KARES TURNİKE

  • The card is tapped; entry time, student and gate are recorded automatically.
  • Late arrivals are marked on the spot, to the minute, against the bell and grace period.
  • Parents get an SMS in the school's name the same morning.
  • Entries are made from a list in the same order as the e-Okul screen; optionally a module fills in the screen.

KARES TURNİKE

How it works

  1. 01

    Step 1: Card is tapped

    The student taps their card at the entrance; the turnstile opens through its own controller, and the entry time and reader are recorded. Exits work the same way.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Late arrivals are marked

    Each class has its bell time and a grace period. A student who taps in after the grace period is marked "late", to the minute.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Absences are detected

    A student who has not tapped in by the time the office sets, and has no excuse on file, is counted absent.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Parents are informed

    An SMS goes out in the school's own name the same morning. Each parent gets at most one message a day; every message sent is logged.

  5. 05

    Step 5: The office gets its report

    Daily absences, the monthly class sheet, the student term report and the e-Okul entry list are ready as Excel with one click.

KARES TURNİKE

What it does

  • No biometric data

    No fingerprints or face recognition; the student card is enough. No special-category personal data is collected and no Turkish ID number is stored.

  • Works when the internet is down

    Records and the attendance calculation live on the office computer at the school. Everything syncs automatically when the connection returns; SMS messages wait in a queue.

  • The software does not open the gate

    The turnstile is opened by its own controller; the software has no open-gate command and only reads the passage records. A deliberate security and liability decision.

  • With your existing turnstile

    Not tied to any brand. For models that cannot be connected directly, a KARES converter board reads the records from the reader line.

  • Windows and web

    A Windows app on the office computer; browser access from the principal's office or from home. The office computer is the only one that talks to the turnstile.

  • Live dashboard

    Present, late, absent and excused counts class by class; a live passage feed, the SMS queue and the turnstile connection status on one screen.

Optional module

e-Okul transfer module

e-Okul has no interface open to outside systems; attendance is entered by hand at every school. This module fills in the screen for the office, and the office checks it and saves.

  • The office logs in to MEBBİS with its own password; the password is never stored anywhere.
  • The e-Okul screen is filled in class by class; the office always presses "Save" itself.
  • Every entry is recorded with screenshots before and after.
  • Not required for the basic setup; entering from the Excel list is always possible.

The module's release date and scope are confirmed during the site visit.

Privacy and security

The data belongs to the school

KARES TURNİKE was designed around how schools actually work and around KVKK (Turkish data protection law): minimal data, clear permissions, every action recorded.

KVKK privacy notice

  • No biometric data and no Turkish ID number is stored; the student card is enough.
  • Records are kept in a cloud project opened in the school's name or entirely on the school's own server; KARES has no access to your data.
  • Parental consent is stored in the system; parents who have not consented get no automatic messages. Consent is collected at registration together with the privacy notice.
  • Administrator, office and teacher permissions are separate; teachers only see the status of their own classes. Permissions are also enforced on the server.
  • Manual corrections require a reason and record who made them; the SMS log can be exported by date range for a KVKK audit.

Frequently asked questions

We already have a turnstile. Can we use it?

Very likely, yes. The controller model is checked during the site visit; if it cannot be connected directly, it is connected through the KARES converter board that reads records from the reader line. The goal is to set up the system without replacing the turnstile.

What if a student gives their card to a friend?

The system reads the card, not the person; that is true of every card-based system. But every passage is recorded to the second, and the office sees anomalies such as two entries with the same card a short time apart in the report. Enforcing card discipline through school rules, without taking on the KVKK burden of biometrics, is the right balance for most schools.

What if a parent does not want SMS messages?

Parental consent is stored in the system; parents who have not consented get no automatic messages. Consent is collected at registration together with the privacy notice.

Do parents still get an SMS if the internet is down?

The message is queued and sent when the connection returns. An "absent" message that arrives in the afternoon loses its meaning, so queued absence messages are cancelled after 12:00; the office sees this on the dashboard and can call the parent if needed.

Can teachers use the system?

Yes, but only to see the attendance status of their own classes. Corrections, excuses and SMS stay with the office; the system enforces this at the permission level.

Does it write to e-Okul automatically?

The basic setup produces an Excel list in the same order as the e-Okul entry screen; the school secretary enters it by reading from the list. With the optional e-Okul transfer module the system fills in the screen, and the office checks it and saves it themselves.

Where is our data stored?

In a cloud project opened in the school's name or, optionally, on the school's own server. Either way the data belongs to the school; KARES has no access to your data.

How long does setup take?

Usually two weeks from the site visit to the first SMS: one day for turnstile installation, an afternoon for card registration, and 2–5 business days for SMS sender name approval.
