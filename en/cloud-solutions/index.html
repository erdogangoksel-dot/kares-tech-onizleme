Cloud Solutions

Where is your data, who can reach it, and does the backup really restore?

Most companies find out the answers when something goes wrong. We review your accounts, backups and internet-facing services from the ground up, report the risks in order of priority, then fix them with you.

Request an assessmentHow it works

Cloud Solutions

Who is it for?

  • Companies without their own IT team

    Businesses whose servers, e-mail and backups are looked after by an employee or an outside helper on the side.

  • Companies planning a move to the cloud

    Companies whose office server is ageing, or who want to move their ERP or files to the cloud or to a hybrid setup.

  • Companies under KVKK and audit pressure

    Accounting, healthcare, education and manufacturing companies that must document where personal data is kept and who can access it.

Cloud Solutions

How it works

  1. 01

    Step 1: Discovery

    With your consent we review accounts, permissions, multi-factor authentication, backups and internet-facing services.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Report

    Findings in order of priority, in non-technical language: what is urgent and what can be planned is spelled out.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Implementation

    We carry out the steps you approve: permission clean-up, authentication, backup routines, migration or hardening.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Monitoring

    If you wish, we monitor the systems regularly and test periodically that backups actually restore.

Cloud Solutions

What it does

  • Account and permission review

    Unused accounts, excessive permissions, shared passwords and admin accounts are checked one by one.

  • Multi-factor authentication

    We check whether multi-factor authentication is on for e-mail, cloud and remote-access accounts, and roll it out.

  • Backups that really restore

    Taking a backup is not enough: where it is stored, whether it is encrypted and whether a restore works are all tested.

  • Internet-facing services

    Remote desktops, databases and admin panels reachable from the internet are found; the ones you do not need are closed.

Cloud Solutions

Our services

  • Cloud security assessment

    A current-state review of accounts, multi-factor authentication, backups and internet-facing services, with a prioritised report.

  • Backup and disaster recovery

    Encrypted backups kept in a separate location, and a written plan for how work gets back up when a server or the office is down.

  • Cloud, hybrid or on-premise

    Moving ERP, files and e-mail to the cloud, to a hybrid setup or to a renewed local server; we choose what suits you together.

  • KVKK-aware hosting

    Regular monitoring of servers, backups and access logs, with an alert to you when something unusual happens.

    Configuring systems with secure settings, plus a short, practical security training for your team.

Backup and disaster recovery

A backup proves its worth on the day it comes back

Backups are encrypted, kept in a separate location and restored on a regular test schedule. What to do when something goes wrong is written down in advance.

Privacy and security

Our own access is limited too

We follow data security rules during the review itself.

KVKK privacy notice

  • Reviews are done only with your written consent and on systems within the agreed scope.
  • Separate, time-limited accounts are used for access and closed when the work is done.
  • Reports contain no personal data or passwords; findings are written at system level.
  • For personal data we recommend data centres in Türkiye or the European Union.

Cloud Solutions

Frequently asked questions

How long does the assessment take, and will it disrupt our work?

It depends on how many systems you have; we agree the scope in the first meeting. The review is done without stopping running systems.

Is the cloud or our own server more secure?

Both are secure when set up correctly and risky when not. We weigh cloud, hybrid and on-premise options together, based on your data, your internet connection and your team.

Which cloud providers do you work with?

We are not tied to a specific provider. We recommend the option that fits your needs, your data-location requirements and your current infrastructure.

We use Mikro, Logo or Netsis. Can you move the ERP too?

Yes. Backing up and moving the ERP server and securing remote access are within our scope. ERP-side work is covered by our ERP solutions.

What is a disaster recovery plan?

A written plan for the order in which work is brought back, from where and by whom, when a server fails, ransomware strikes or the office cannot be reached. We confirm the plan with regular restore tests.
