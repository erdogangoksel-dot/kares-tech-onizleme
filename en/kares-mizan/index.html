Trial balance analysis, reconciliation and year-end checks

Figures are calculated, not generated.

Trial balance checks, customer, supplier and bank reconciliation and year-end preparation are still done in Excel and by hand in most places. KARES MİZAN reads the data from the program you use, calculates the trial balance to the last cent, finds problems with rule-based checks and explains each finding in plain language. Decisions and approval stay with you.

KARES MİZAN

Whose work does it make easier?

  • Accounting firms

    For firms working with many companies: each company is separate, and the team works with roles. Trial balance checks and the year-end list run in the same order, company by company.

  • In-house accounting and finance teams

    Check your trial balance before month-end and year-end closing; see customer, supplier and bank differences before the filing period.

  • Business owners and managers

    See the trial balance, findings and reports of the companies shared with you, with read-only access; findings are written so that someone without accounting jargon can follow them.

KARES MİZAN

How it works

  1. 01

    Step 1: Connect the data

    Upload e-Ledger (e-Defter) XML files or an Excel/CSV trial balance, sub-ledger, voucher list and chart of accounts; set up a direct read connection for Mikro and Logo.

  2. 02

    Step 2: The trial balance is calculated

    Trial balance, sub-ledgers and balances are calculated by an engine with defined rules. The same data always gives the same result; AI produces no figures.

  3. 03

    Step 3: The checks run

    Rule-based checks list findings on topics such as balancing, reverse balances, cash, customer and supplier accounts and VAT carry-forward; each finding is explained in plain language.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Reconciliation and year-end

    Reconcile customer, supplier and bank accounts, follow the year-end checklist and calendar; get draft reports and pre-filing comparisons.

KARES MİZAN

What it does

  • Figures are calculated, not generated

    Every figure in the trial balance, findings and reports comes from a calculation engine with defined rules. With AI on or off, the result is the same to the last cent.

  • Reads from the program you use

    e-Ledger XML (journal and general ledger), Excel/CSV trial balance, sub-ledger, voucher list and chart of accounts; Mikro REST and Logo Objects REST (Tiger, GO, Wings) connections.

  • Reads only, never writes

    No record is written to your ERP or accounting program. Findings are kept only as notes; the source record does not change.

  • Rule-based checks

    Trial balance and voucher balancing, journal–ledger consistency, accounts with reverse balances, negative cash balance, undefined account codes, customer and supplier accounts running the wrong way, the VAT carry-forward chain, bank balance differences. Checks can be switched on or off per company.

  • Plain-language explanations

    For each finding, the likely cause and the suggested fix are written in plain language. The figures in the explanation come from the calculation engine, not from AI.

  • It suggests, you approve

    For unrecognised Excel columns or sub-accounts that cannot be classified, AI suggests a mapping; the suggestion is not applied until you approve it.

  • Customer and supplier reconciliation

    Compare customer and supplier balances, see differences per account and follow the reconciliation status in one place.

  • Bank reconciliation

    The bank account balance is compared with the statement closing balance; differences and unmatched transactions are listed.

  • Year-end checklist and calendar

    Year-end closing steps and dates are followed per company; open tasks and findings appear on one screen.

  • Draft financial statements and ratio analysis

    A draft balance sheet and income statement from the trial balance, with basic ratio analysis. Every output carries an information note.

  • Tax return pre-check

    The return totals you enter for VAT, provisional tax and withholding tax are compared with the trial balance; differences show before filing.

  • Draft TFRS, BOBİ FRS and KÜMİ FRS conversion

    Draft adjusting entries and draft statements from the VUK (Turkish tax procedure law) trial balance to TFRS, BOBİ FRS or KÜMİ FRS. The outputs are working papers, not an audit opinion.

  • Multi-company and role-based

    Many companies under one firm or company account; administrator, team member and read-only user roles are separate.

What it does, what it does not

A helper tool: it reads, calculates, flags and reports.

KARES MİZAN does not replace your accounting program and produces no legal records. Its limits are clear from the start.

Does

  • Only reads data from the program or file you use.
  • Calculates the trial balance, sub-ledgers and balances so that they give the same result every time.
  • Flags findings and explains them in plain language; it never touches the source record.
  • Prepares draft financial statements, ratio analysis and pre-filing comparisons.

Does not

  • Writes no record to your ERP or accounting program.
  • Produces no legal books, berat files or tax return files; you or your accounting professional file the return.
  • Issues no e-Invoice or e-Archive documents.
  • Gives no audit opinion; the TFRS conversion stays a draft.

Important information

  • Reports are for information only; responsibility for financial statements and tax returns lies with the authorised accounting professional.
  • TFRS outputs are drafts; they have not been independently audited.
  • Mikro and Logo are registered trademarks of their owners; KARES MİZAN is not a product of these vendors. Connections use the interfaces the vendors publish, for reading only.

Privacy and security

Your data is yours, you stay in control

KARES MİZAN is a multi-tenant web application that runs in the browser. Each firm and company sees only its own data.

KVKK privacy notice

  • Access to the ERP and accounting program is for reading only; no write permission is used.
  • No tax ID (VKN), national ID (TCKN) or IBAN is sent to the AI service.
  • Who changed what, and when: every action is kept in an audit log that cannot be altered.
  • Roles are separate: administrator, team member and read-only user.
  • Two-step verification is mandatory for administrator accounts.

Frequently asked questions

Does AI calculate the trial balance?

No. Trial balance, sub-ledgers and balances are calculated by an engine with defined rules; the same data always gives the same result. AI only explains findings and suggests column or account mappings; suggestions are not applied until you approve them.

Does it write anything to our ERP or accounting program?

No. KARES MİZAN only reads. Findings and reconciliation notes are kept inside KARES MİZAN; your source records do not change.

Which programs does it work with?

With e-Ledger XML (journal and general ledger) and Excel/CSV files it works regardless of the program you use. There are direct read connections for Mikro (REST) and Logo (Objects REST: Tiger, GO, Wings).

Does KARES MİZAN file the tax return?

No. Before filing, KARES MİZAN compares the return totals you enter with the trial balance and shows the differences. It produces no return file; you or your authorised accounting professional file the return.

Does it replace the accountant?

No. KARES MİZAN is a helper tool: it checks, flags and reports. Responsibility for financial statements and tax returns lies with the authorised accounting professional.

Is the TFRS conversion ready for audit?

No. The outputs of the conversion from VUK to TFRS, BOBİ FRS or KÜMİ FRS are draft working papers; they have not been independently audited and do not replace an audit opinion.

How much of our data does the AI see?

Only as much as an explanation or suggestion needs. Tax IDs (VKN), national IDs (TCKN) and IBANs are not sent to the AI service; the figures stay in the calculation engine.
