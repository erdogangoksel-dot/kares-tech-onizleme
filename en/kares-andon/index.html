Factory floor alert and response

Every minute of downtime: seen, routed, solved.

On many factory floors a stopped station is still reported by shouting, walking over or a light nobody is watching. KARES ANDON turns every fault into a routed, timed and accountable ticket that reaches the right team's phones. It runs on standard tablets and phones, with no PLC wiring needed to start.

KARES ANDON

How it works

  1. 01

    Step 1: Trigger

    The operator taps a category on the station tablet: material, machine fault, quality or safety. The alert is saved locally first and the station turns yellow on the board.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Route

    A ticket is created and a notification with the machine and location reaches the phones of the responsible team.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Accept

    The first engineer to accept owns the ticket; the operator sees who is coming and the escalation timer stops.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Resolve

    A short guided report records the root cause and the action taken, and the station returns to green.

KARES ANDON

What it does

  • Glove-friendly operator screen

    Large touch targets, high contrast and no typing: raising an alert is a single tap on a category.

  • Works when the Wi-Fi drops

    Every alert is written to the tablet's local queue before it is sent. If the network drops, the alert waits and is sent in order when the connection returns.

  • Routing by skill

    Notifications go to the right team (maintenance, electrical, quality), not to whoever is nearest, with sound and vibration profiles meant for a noisy floor.

  • One owner per ticket

    The first engineer to accept gets the job; the server makes sure there is exactly one owner, so nobody makes a wasted trip.

  • Automatic escalation

    [DRAFT] An alert left unacknowledged for 60 seconds goes to the shift supervisor; one unresolved after 5 minutes goes to the engineering manager. Every step is logged.

  • Live andon board

    Green, yellow and red status for every station on a TV above the floor. The board shows no personal data.

  • Root cause on every ticket

    A ticket cannot be closed without the root cause and the action taken, so the data is usable from the first week.

  • MTTR and downtime analytics

    Mean time to repair, root-cause frequency, downtime trends and response times, with daily and weekly views and CSV export.

  • Machine integration later

    [DRAFT] Next on the roadmap: an edge gateway that reads PLCs (Modbus, OPC UA) so machine faults open tickets on their own, built on the same platform without rework.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to connect our machines or PLCs?

No. The first stage runs on tablets and phones with manual alerts. Connecting machines is a later, optional step on the same platform.

What if the factory Wi-Fi is unreliable?

Alerts are stored on the tablet first and sent in order when the connection is back, so an alert is never lost because the network dropped.

Can we change the escalation rules?

Yes. Who is notified and after how long is set per plant together with your team during setup.

How do we start?

With a single pilot line: we set up the operator tablet, the engineer app and the andon board for one area, configure your machines and escalation rules, and measure the response times together.
